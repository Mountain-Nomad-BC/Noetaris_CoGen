# Privacy notice

Version: 2026-10-17

Data use, user rights and outstanding deployment-specific decisions.

## Controller and contact

Noetaris CoGen is an independent, experimental, noncommercial AMD Developer Hackathon project operated in the United States under the Mountain Nomad BC name. This project name is not asserted to be a registered legal entity. The public privacy/support contact is pending confirmation and no business/service address was provided. Any legally required operator identity, address, representative or officer disclosure remains for review; no residential address is published.

## Data categories and purposes

The design processes canonical account identifiers and provider-subject bindings for authentication, session-secret hashes for session control, requested source files/text/metadata for retrieval, extracted semantic/index records for search, questions and responses for research history, and bounded security/audit records for operations. Provider passwords are never requested. Login provider tokens are transient and must not be persisted as application credentials.

## Legal bases and sources

The controller must record the appropriate legal basis for each purpose, such as necessary contract performance, a documented legitimate-interest assessment or valid consent where applicable. This draft does not assume consent for every purpose. Data may be supplied by you, your authorized organization or the authentication provider. Special-category processing requires a separate lawful condition and is not authorized in this preview.

## Recipients and international transfers

The intended architecture includes Google/Firebase hosting, an OCI-hosted API/database, Cloudflare R2 and optional AMD/OpenRouter inference and Evolus. Neon is an optional undeployed fallback. This list is intended architecture, not a statement that all providers receive your content. The controller must confirm actual processors, subprocessors, regions, contracts, retention and lawful transfer mechanisms before activation. Do not assume EU-only processing or provider non-training terms without verification.

## Retention and deletion

The current retention operation is inspection-only: automatic cleanup and document deletion are not enabled, and originals and audit events are retained. Documents, derived records and chat history may therefore remain until an authorized removal process is implemented and executed. Browser login sessions default to 12 hours, configurable within 1-24 hours; expiration or revocation does not itself promise deletion of stored session rows. Owner-held backups have no qualified automatic expiry. Access revocation is not physical erasure. Final category-specific retention periods, deletion execution, backup handling and response procedures remain unresolved; no immediate-erasure or zero-retention claim is made.

## Your rights

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability, object to qualifying processing, and withdraw consent without affecting prior lawful processing. Rights have legal conditions and exceptions. You may complain to your competent supervisory authority. Requests need a verified private contact and proportionate identity checking; never post identity documents publicly. An operational request channel and response procedure must be commissioned.

## AI, profiling and consequential decisions

The application retrieves document passages and may use generative AI to draft answers. This can expose inaccuracies or inferred information. It is not offered as an automated system for legal or similarly significant decisions about people. A generic AI disclaimer does not resolve GDPR automated-decision duties or establish lawful processing. Such a use would need separate assessment, safeguards and approval.

## Cookies, security and optional services

Browser authentication uses Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite session cookies and anti-CSRF protections when configured. Local fallback credentials remain in memory rather than persistent browser storage. Canonical UUIDs are identifiers, not proof of anonymity or compliance. Current local Docker testing uses loopback HTTP and restricted internal networks; production HTTPS and encryption-at-rest/backup protections must be separately verified. No universal encryption guarantee is made. No optional analytics/advertising consent is inferred; any added nonessential tracking requires its own assessment and appropriate controls.

## Children and sensitive information

The public demonstration is for adults aged 18 or older; Google/GitHub registration is intended to be open without invitations. Do not submit children’s data, health records, biometric information or other sensitive content. Public personal-data upload admission remains disabled until the necessary privacy, identity and access acceptance is complete.
