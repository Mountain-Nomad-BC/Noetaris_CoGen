# AI transparency

Version: 2026-10-17

Understand where AI is used and how to assess its outputs.

## You are interacting with AI

Chat and research answers may be generated or assisted by AI. AI can make mistakes, invent details, omit context or reproduce bias. Verify claims against the original cited source before relying on or sharing an answer. The interface must identify generated responses rather than presenting them as independently verified facts.

## Retrieval is not training

Uploading and indexing a document extracts searchable text and semantic representations. It does not, by itself, train or permanently teach the underlying model. A source becoming queryable does not establish truth, rights clearance or review approval.

## Providers and human review

The selected hosted inference family is Gemma, with AMD as intended primary and exact free OpenRouter variants as intended fallback. CPU Gemma currently serves the qualified local workflow. MiniLM supports retrieval. External dispatch and fallback require qualified authorization and accounting; provider availability varies. Human review is a separate recorded action, not a consequence of generation.

## Publishing and consequential use

Preserve AI-origin disclosures when publishing generated material. Do not pass generated evidence or synthetic media off as an authentic record. No qualified deepfake, biometric categorization or emotion-recognition service is offered. Consequential decisions require appropriate human judgment and any separately applicable safeguards.

## Legal scope

GDPR privacy and automated-decision obligations and EU AI Act transparency obligations are distinct. Applicable roles, exceptions, machine-readable marking and deployment-specific duties need assessment. This notice and a visible AI label alone do not certify compliance. See the operator privacy notice and internal readiness review.
